class ordered_control

#class ordered_control

The shape of a B-tree: each node's children, key count and subtree size, and the free nodes.

kl::ordered_map and kl::ordered_set hold one each beside their own arrays. Use them rather than this directly.

#public key_moves thicken( u64 parent, u64 c, out u64 entered )

Before entering child c of parent, gives it more than ORDERED_MIN keys by borrowing from a sibling or merging with one. entered is the child now holding what child c held.

#public u64 descend_remove( u64 node, u64 c )

Counts a key about to leave from under node, and answers its child c. A root a merge has emptied is freed instead, and the child becomes the root.

#public key_run remove_from_leaf( u64 leaf, u64 j )
