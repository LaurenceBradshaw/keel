module kl::complex

struct complex

#struct complex<T> implements equatable where T : floating

A complex number, re + im i, of f32 or f64 parts.

A complex copies like a number. kl::complex<f64> { 1.0, 2.0 } is 1 + 2i, and kl::complex<f64> {} is 0. The arithmetic takes a complex or a T on either side, and the rest are methods: z.abs(), z.conj(). A collection of them is a kl::complex<T>[N], a run or a list; complex data in an ndarray is the caller's ndarray<T, 2, S...>, real parts then imaginary.
