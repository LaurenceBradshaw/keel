class ring

#class ring<T, u64 N> implements iterable<T>, fixed_memory<T>

A double-ended queue of at most N elements of T, held inline: it never allocates.

Elements are added and removed at either end in constant time, and indexed from 0 at the front. Pushing onto a full ring panics, try_push_back hands the element back, and push_back_evict makes room by removing the element at the other end. Dropping the ring drops every element, back to front.
